
Your bookmarks, as a feed. A personal, TikTok-style vertical feed for the posts
you save across your socials — played back as an installable, offline PWA and
synced to your phone over your own Wi-Fi.

- `shared/`    — the Item/Bundle/Scraper contract
- `web/`       — the PWA player (vertical scroll-snap feed, X + YouTube cards)
- `companion/` — local HTTPS server (serves the PWA + bundle, one-tap LAN sync)
- `extension/` — in-browser scraper (next)
